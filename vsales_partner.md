# Varidian Sales Partner Commission Structure & Agreement

## Programme overview

Varidian Sales Partners earn a commission on every client they bring to Varidian Consulting Limited, paid once that client pays. Partners are independent: no salary, no fixed hours, and no targets they can be penalised for.

- **Partners** open doors, introduce Varidian, and build the client relationship.
- **Varidian** runs demos, scopes the work, sets the price, signs the client and delivers.
- **Commission** is a share of what the client actually pays, as set out in the next section.

## Commission structure

Commission is calculated on the amount the client pays, excluding VAT, third-party costs (hardware, SMS credits, payment gateway fees) and refunds.

| Revenue type | Examples | Commission | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Software subscription (SaaS) | HostelHub, Coursify, Varidian Events, VaridianSMS, Varidian Reach | 20% of subscription fees | First 12 months of the client's subscription |
| Once-off licence or setup fee | BizManager or Varidian Books licence, installation, training | 15% | Once, when paid |
| Custom software project | Bespoke system built for a client | 10% of project value | Paid in step with each client instalment |
| Hosting and domains | Varidian Hosting plans | 15% | First 12 months |
| Referral only | Partner passes on a lead but takes no further part | 5% of the first payment | Once |

**Payment terms**

1. Commission is earned only after Varidian receives the client's payment in full for that invoice.
2. Varidian pays commission by the 10th of the following month, by mobile money or bank transfer.
3. Each payment comes with a statement showing the client, the amount received and the commission due.
4. If a client is refunded, the commission on that amount is deducted from the partner's next payment.
5. Partners are responsible for declaring their own commission income for tax purposes.

## Lead registration and client ownership

The first partner to register a lead in writing owns that lead for 90 days.

1. **Register the lead** by sending Lumas the organisation's name, contact person and the product or service of interest (WhatsApp or email is fine).
2. **Varidian confirms** within 2 working days, or flags that the client is already registered or an existing Varidian client.
3. **The lead stays yours for 90 days.** If no meeting or proposal happens in that time, the lead becomes open again unless both sides agree to extend it.
4. **Existing Varidian clients** don't earn commission, except on a new product or project the partner introduces.
5. **If two partners work a client together**, they agree a split before the proposal goes out; if they can't agree, commission is split equally.
6. **Disputes** are settled by the Director, whose decision is final.
